Open index.html directly, or start a local server in this directory:
python3 -m http.server 8777 --bind 127.0.0.1
Then open http://127.0.0.1:8777

The complete issue is one continuous sequence of 32 numbered pages.
All five editorial translations, eight features, atlas annotations and
three conference inserts are printed in the reading flow. There are no
article branches, hidden language variants or document popups.

The contents and printed “See page…” references quietly jump to their
numbered sheet. Previous / Next, the complete page selector, arrow keys
and Print issue belong to the viewer outside the magazine paper.
Old article URLs still land on the corresponding numbered page.

Pages 3–12: editorial, English / Spanish / French / Italian / German
Pages 13–20: sea, architecture, energy and mobility
Pages 21–22: island atlas and printed island annotations
Pages 23–26: cooperative finance and architectural portraits
Pages 27–29: programme, delegate credential and cooperative certificate
Pages 30–31: closing editorial
Page 32: present-day edition notes / provenance

No installation, accounts, external fonts or network services required.
The magazine works offline. Nothing has been published or deployed.

appear as imagined contributors, without authentic quotes or claims of
attendance. See page 32 and ART-DIRECTION.txt for detailed provenance.
