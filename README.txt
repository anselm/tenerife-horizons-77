The complete issue is one continuous sequence of 36 numbered pages.
All five editorial translations, eight features, atlas annotations and
three conference inserts are printed in the reading flow. There are no
article branches, hidden language variants or document popups.

The contents and printed “See page…” references quietly jump to their
numbered sheet. Previous / Next, the complete page selector, arrow keys
and Print issue belong to the viewer outside the magazine paper.
Old article URLs still land on the corresponding numbered page.
