# 2026-10-22 — People at the table

Point-in-time entry written on 2026-10-22 from the conversation, source text and commits. Follows [the magazine’s change of form](20261022-from-retrospective-to-magazine.md).

The conference became interesting through the encounters it might have made possible. Real people enter an imagined gathering at a moment when something in their work is changing. Their historical work anchors the year; the attendance, conversations and Tenerife influences are fiction, explained at the end rather than interrupting the magazine.

Christopher Alexander’s four-page essay turns on a fisherman’s quiet guidance through Bocacangrejo. The village teaches through stairs, shared edges, white houses, effort and daily encounters. “What we build builds us in turn” is an invented line for this essay. His proposed machine evaluation of patterns remains tied to particular people and landscapes. The notes acknowledge the real 1970 book draft and 1979 publication rather than claiming Tenerife originated his thinking.

Cousteau’s underwater viewing room gives attention time to become care. Goodall’s new institute opens the drawing to other lives. Gilliam, in the year of *Jabberwocky*, gives imagined rooms the fingerprints of use and repair. Their contributions keep the humor small and situated.

A search for a voice whose 1977 was truly pivotal led to Wangari Maathai and the founding of the Green Belt Movement. Her feature asks about water, work, tending and who returns after the visitors leave. Habib Rahman’s dismissal from Delhi’s Urban Art Commission gives a shorter contribution its difficult question: who can refuse a bad proposal? The two pieces alter the conference’s conversation rather than simply adding names to a roster.

The technical proposals also became more particular. Personal aircraft rise in skyscraper lift stations and glide down toward a destination, repeating the lift where necessary. The station supplies the height; gravity is not free energy.

No Arabic-language contribution has yet been added. Hassan Fathy remains a possible direction, distinct from the South Asian perspectives discussed around Habib Rahman and Habib Fida Ali.
