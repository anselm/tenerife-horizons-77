# Specific islands and a common sea

An underwater-habitat spread became a way to bring the premise into focus. Submarine trams would connect islands; a little Italian could sit beside English and Spanish; an imagined architectural contribution could ask how living beneath the water might renew a relationship with nature.

The useful correction was concrete: “don't have fish in the air,” and name La Palma instead of saying “other islands.” The drawings needed geographic and physical conviction. Less cartoon, more architectural proposition; fewer generic promises, more particular places.

That feedback remains a test for the later edition. Even an extravagant proposal has to belong somewhere. Its animals should be in the sea.
