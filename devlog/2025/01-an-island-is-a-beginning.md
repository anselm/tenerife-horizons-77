# An island is a beginning

The premise was a magazine from a dazzling international futures conference on Tenerife in 1977: architecture, undersea habitats, new forms of living, and the distant future. A meeting which never happened, but whose enthusiasm should feel entirely possible.

The early imagined horizon was 2030 and beyond. The attraction was the distance between what the delegates could earnestly imagine and what a later reader knows. The humor needed to live inside that distance. No knowing wink was required.

The island mattered as a place: Teide, steep slopes, the Atlantic, other islands with names, and different languages sharing the page. This was never meant to be a generic space-age resort.
