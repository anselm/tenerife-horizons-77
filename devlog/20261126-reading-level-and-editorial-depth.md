# Reading level and editorial depth

26 November 2026. Analysis requested by Anselm; the sample prose below is a proposal, not an applied rewrite. Read with the [editorial compass](20261120-editorial-and-design-compass.md). The new page assembly and upright headings are a separate implemented change: [earlier contributed sheets](20261126-earlier-contributed-sheets.md).

## Finding

The sampled English prose scores **7.5 on Flesch–Kincaid**, with individual essays or sections between **5.8 and 10.2**. This describes its verbal surface, not the sophistication of its ideas. The characteristic construction is a concrete scene, several short declarative sentences, a rhetorical question, then a compact moral observation. That serves the lived recollections well. Repeated across theoretical contributions, it makes different minds sound like the same lucid editorial narrator.

The strongest opportunity is to let a few papers carry sustained arguments: define a distinction, follow its consequences, entertain an objection, and leave a consequential difficulty unresolved. Longer sentences then have something to do. The magazine can contain demanding papers alongside a plain kitchen notice, a memory of swimming and a ridiculous drawing.

Treat **17–19 as an experimental numerical range for selected analytical passages**, not a validated postgraduate standard or a requirement for every page. The formula cannot establish postgraduate knowledge, originality or intellectual depth. Its inputs are sentence length and syllables per word. A [1980 Army research review](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo44433/pdf/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo44433.pdf) explicitly cautions that writing to a score can displace attention from readers' needs. That later methodological reference belongs here, not in the fictional 1977 magazine.

## Measurement

Source: the magazine's exported article bodies, not PDF extraction. Nineteen English essay/section samples, **7,827 words, 577 sentences, 11,802 syllables**. Whole foreign-language paragraphs were omitted; names, brief multilingual phrases and titles remain. This is a selected English corpus, not a score for the entire multilingual proceedings. Headings, captions, notices, edition notes and the distinct foreign-language openings were excluded.

Measured with `textstat 0.7.13`, English defaults and its CMU pronunciation dictionary support. HTML was stripped, entities decoded and paragraph whitespace normalised. The formula is `0.39 × words/sentences + 11.8 × syllables/words − 15.59`. The combined score was calculated on the combined text, not by averaging the rounded grades. Proper names, punctuation and tokenisation introduce estimation error; another implementation may differ. The initial rough pass included three foreign-language paragraphs; removing them left the rounded combined score unchanged.

## Where to deepen the argument

1. **Alexander, IV / A fitness for life — first priority.** The score is proposed, but its most difficult implication remains largely unstated: different goods may not be interchangeable. Can communal sociability compensate for lost privacy? Does a high aggregate score conceal one person's exclusion? Distinguish a quantity to improve from a condition no improvement elsewhere may override. Keep the fisherman, the bucket and the creatures in the account; retain the closing return to the white wall and stair. The proposed machine can search arrangements, but the inhabitants must be able to contest the measure itself.
2. **Credit for things that last — second priority.** The questions about departure and inheritance are excellent beginnings. Follow one through: repay a resident's improvements without enabling speculation in scarce land; provide a means of leaving without putting the trust at the mercy of immediate redemption. Describe an institutional choice and its cost. Preserve the Friday joke and the landlord with longer meetings.
3. **Alexander, III / The necessary effort, with the Italian opening.** The distinction between effort that exhausts and effort that joins needs a test. Shared work can cultivate reciprocity while falling disproportionately on those least free to refuse it. Ask whose time is counted and who may withdraw. This develops the existing concern for people unable to climb, rather than replacing affection for the village with a lecture against it. Do not apply English FK to the Italian contribution.
4. **Goodall / A place for other lives.** The existing passage already refuses to reduce animals to profitable resources. Develop the tension between local human authority and lives which cannot represent themselves at a committee. What happens when obligations conflict? Let care remain more demanding than either sentimental identification or an administrative solution.
5. **Rahman / The right to refuse.** This is the highest-scoring sampled essay already, but could sustain a clearer distinction between consultation and actual power to refuse. Who protects the examiner's independence; how is that examiner, in turn, answerable? The ending asks the right question. A short preceding argument would give it greater force.
6. **Energy and personal flight.** Move from a principle to a bounded comparison: what is included in the account, what assumptions matter, and under what conditions might the apparent saving disappear? Distinguish individual vehicle efficiency from the obligations of the whole transport network. No invented performance figures; retain the imaginative prospect of spending the afternoon's height on a journey home.
7. **Fuller and Snelson / Three seats at the table.** Develop the working relationship as a means of discovery: the artist's material experiment and the system-builder's generalisation expose different errors. Their disagreement can generate knowledge without becoming proof of uncomplicated harmony. Preserve the tension-and-compression image and avoid retrospective reputation.

## What the prose can do differently

Allow more sentences to contain a claim and the condition under which it holds. Let paragraphs accumulate an argument rather than repeatedly reset to a new observation. Use precise terms when they enable a distinction—reciprocity, representation, obligation, speculation, institutional independence—not merely as replacements for ordinary words. Carry an objection beyond its introduction. Give contributors different modes of reasoning: a proposal, a counterexample, a disputation, a tentative working definition.

Do not make every paragraph end in an aphorism. Some can end with evidence, an unresolved consequence or a specific next experiment. This would help the intellectual variety as much as longer sentences would. Keep short sentences where they deliver an emotional or comic turn.

## Two sample revisions

These are newly composed fictional editorial trials, not authentic statements by the named historical participants. They stay within the magazine's 1977 horizon. Scores on a single paragraph are especially unstable; these examples demonstrate a direction, not a whole-essay attainment.

### Alexander: who determines fitness?

The present passage begins, “Such a score would have to be made here, with these people, in this landscape.” Its participatory principle is strong; the revision makes the conflict among participants explicit.

> Such a score would have to be made here, with these people, in this landscape, because the relationships which sustain one household may impose intolerable obligations upon another. Before combining them into a single measure, we should ask whether an improvement in communal life can compensate for the disappearance of solitude, or whether certain freedoms must remain conditions of the experiment rather than quantities available for exchange. The fisherman must help decide what counts; so must the person who waits at home, whose apparent availability may conceal the labour on which everybody else's independence depends. And the account must acknowledge lives which cannot attend its deliberations: the rock, the tide, the creatures whose shore we share.

**Trial FK grade: 16.1.** The added depth comes from distinguishing shared benefit from individual freedom and recognising hidden labour. It remains below the proposed numerical postgraduate range; that is not a reason to pad it.

### Finance: the right to leave

The present paragraph asks how a resident leaves and who inherits a right of use. The revision begins to work through the contradiction rather than stopping at the questions.

> There are difficult questions about departure and inheritance, since a right of use which cannot be surrendered without hardship may become a means of confinement, while a right which can be sold to the highest bidder restores the speculation the trust was established to resist. The constitution must therefore distinguish the departing resident's claim upon improvements made through labour from a claim upon the increasing scarcity of land, and explain how the first can be honoured without financing it through the second. Otherwise the cooperative may become a landlord with longer meetings, distributing the advantages of membership to those already inside while requiring each newcomer to subsidise their security.

**Trial FK grade: 19.1.** This reaches the proposed numerical range because its longer clauses carry a connected institutional argument. It is denser than the existing voice; test it in context before adopting the register more widely.

## A proportionate first revision

Start with Alexander IV and finance: two or three analytical paragraphs in each, preserving their openings and endings. Then develop Rahman and Goodall where the arguments intersect. Re-score each complete essay and assess the actual reading experience; a paragraph scoring 19 does not make its surrounding essay grade 19. Let new material earn additional pages rather than squeeze the digest.

Keep the English arrival, coastal recollections, Oleaje, the fisherman, friendship passages, Gilliam's interruptions and ordinary notices substantially direct. Their clarity is part of the contrast that makes the more demanding papers believable. A uniform postgraduate register would work against the several-contributors premise. No score-driven rewrite has been applied to the magazine in this pass.
