# 2026-10-22 — From retrospective to magazine

Point-in-time entry written on 2026-10-22. Sources: Anselm’s resumed conversation, this repository’s implementation, and its Git history. Continues the [2025 premise](20250330-an-island-is-a-beginning.md).

The project resumed with an ask for a complete web artifact. An initial retrospective archive gave way to a much more precise object: a magazine assembled at the close of the conference, as though it had dropped into our world from another timeline.

The decisive feedback was that announcing the fiction too quickly spoiled the reader’s question: did this really happen? Modern interpretation moved to the final edition notes. The magazine itself speaks from 1977.

A branching reading structure also gave way to one continuous numbered issue. Every article, translation, diagram, atlas note and loose insert is in the flow. Printed “see page” references carry quiet links; navigation and printing controls sit outside the paper. English, Spanish, French, Italian and German share the issue.

The future moved from 2030 to 2027: exactly fifty years after the conference. That correction had to reach the cover, essays, artwork labels and translations. Versioned asset references addressed a stale preview which still showed the old cover text.

The physical conceit is made with aged paper colors, grain, worn edges and a centre fold. Restoring the seam mattered: it helped the screen feel like an old object. The implementation stays buildless, with local assets and no external font dependency.

The project lives in its own 2025 directory. It was published, with Anselm’s approval, as [anselm/tenerife-horizons-77](https://github.com/anselm/tenerife-horizons-77), with [GitHub Pages](https://anselm.github.io/tenerife-horizons-77/) serving the issue.
