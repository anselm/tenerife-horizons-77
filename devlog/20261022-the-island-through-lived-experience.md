# 2026-10-22 — The island through lived experience

Point-in-time entry written on 2026-10-22. Grounded in Anselm’s recollections shared for the magazine and the resulting source text. These memories are adapted into the imagined period, not offered as documentary evidence of 1977 events.

The island entered the work through an afternoon spent swimming and freediving at Montaña Amarilla: glittering waves, barracuda below, and no return to shore until the afternoon had gone. The account’s emotional centre is the temporary ordinariness of being fishes with flippers and goggles.

Another swim beneath fish-farm cages brought a dolphin, large lazy turtles and rays. Anselm corrected the register from illegality to small transgression. Small whales on other swims and strange tiny creatures in quiet water widened the scale of attention. No whale-shark encounter was added.

Cliffside homes, dramatic changes of landscape and African dust arriving as calima joined the same notebook. The fisherman’s knowledge remained something shown in action and interpreted by a visitor. A short note acknowledges conquest, enslavement, assimilation and Indigenous persistence, without claiming the fictional fisherman’s ancestry.

A pale Andalusian stallion brought from Seville made room for another small essay. Formal schooling gives way to learning in which the animal’s willingness can change the plan. The remembered fall and tangled rope preserve the animal’s strength alongside his gentleness. The supplied contemporary photograph is identified as such in the final notes.

The first barracuda image felt too detailed, too much like a 2026 image. A simpler illustration with broader washes and a limited print palette replaced it. Visual restraint helped the scene return to the magazine’s period.

The marine article supplied later describes a 2023 expedition using a rebuilt 1970s submersible. It is linked as further reading in the notes; its modern operations are not transplanted into 1977. The friends’ submarine work has not been turned into a dated historical claim.
