# Assembled pages; remaining inside 1977

26 November 2026. Current follow-up to the [design compass](20261120-editorial-and-design-compass.md). This records the final implementation, not the abandoned draft.

## The decision that matters

The magazine speaks from inside 1977. An initial archival timeline used retrospective language and placed later actual events after the reveal. Anselm rejected that framing: the humour comes from inhabiting the hopes, not explaining them from the future. The conference file now precedes the reveal. The 1982 EPCOT opening and the later Cairo/Nairobi meetings were removed from its prose; modern source labels and compilation dates were removed too. They are not information the fictional delegates possess.

The future entries are restricted to verified plans: the UN decided in December 1976 on a 1979 science-and-technology conference, with preparation beginning early in 1977; WMO decided in May–June 1977 to convene a February 1979 meeting in Geneva. The science-and-technology entry does not backdate the eventual Vienna venue. Disney’s exhibition proposals were public in 1975 and developing in 1976–77. Tomorrowland was already open. The distinction is between what has happened, what is being proposed, and what remains unsettled.

## A material method

Inspected [Jericho, 1 June 1976](https://habitat.scarp.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Jericho_Issue2_01061976.pdf). Large contrasting headlines, a photograph and dense small reports coexist. The scan demonstrates its visual structure, not the exact production process: do not infer photocopying merely from its appearance. [Cambridge’s account of punk fanzines](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/writing-politics-in-modern-britain/writing-from-below/B2014902595FA2E78E1943E2828B1CB5) describes period cut-and-paste and photocopy methods. A paste-up master could also be printed by offset. Preserve that distinction.

This is a bounded trial, not a whole-magazine redesign. It introduces separately measured slips, typewritten dates, condensed numerals, italic display type, spare rules, restrained paper tones and one attached notice. The plans page contrasts a short report, a prospectus and a meeting notice. Print body type stays at 9.4 pt; no repeated art was added. The initial single-page chronology was too cramped, so the material gained room rather than smaller type.

For the deeper pass, work contribution by contribution: duplicated secretariat bulletins, a corrected line, an expense slip, a Spanish notice, a photographic contact strip, letters from opposing voices. Let the colour architectural perspectives coexist with cheaper-looking sheets. A join should reveal a real addition or correction, not act as wallpaper. Do not make every page dirty or arbitrarily rotate its reading text.

## The purpose of the optimism

Anselm’s note, received during this work, preserved verbatim:

> part of what this imaginary project is is a grounding touchstone, in a way it is a homage to the optimism of futurism, i also noticed that it is an homage to friendship, and perhaps there is a syncretic optimism in how friends imagine and dream about future possibilities - even if those don't always work out.

Editorial consequence: the history supplies a setting for shared imagining, not a prediction scorecard. Neither technological triumph nor knowing disappointment is the organising story. The proposals can remain unfinished while the encounters and friendships matter. Keep this in conversation with [the dreamers note](20261120-the-dreamers.md), without inserting a repeated statement of the theme into every essay.
