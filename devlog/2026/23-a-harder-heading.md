# A harder heading

28 October 2026

The rounded display face followed one strand of seventies typography too far. Anselm wanted a harder architectural voice: geometric, tightly set capitals. URW Gothic Demi now carries the principal headlines and masthead, with quieter sentence-case continuation headings and Roman essay text. The website and three print formats share the change.

The fisherman arrives with neither a conference paper nor newspaper-wrapped fish. The screening-room fragment gives Warhol and Renan a small shared moment: a film of net repair, a request to see it again, and the possibility that the people who receive films might also make them. Their visit and responses remain fictional. Warhol’s actual Hammer and Sickle work predates this imagined encounter.

Reference: [The Andy Warhol Museum](https://www.warhol.org/lessons/hammer-and-sickle/); [Monotype’s Avant Garde history](https://www.myfonts.com/a/font/content/font-field-guide/itc-avant-garde-gothic).

A second pass uses the largest empty areas for four short workshop leaves: the last hundred metres of an ocean journey, a winter energy account, a repair reserve, and a test of the proposed architectural scoring system. These appear in both editions; the paper edition places them only where the remaining space accommodates them.
