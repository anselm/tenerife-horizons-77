# Natural word spacing

31 October 2026

Anselm spotted stretched spaces in the digest’s narrow columns, particularly around source domains, and noted that the issue extended beyond the closing notes. The print paragraph style selected justification by font size; enlarging the digest’s working type had brought even its notes into that range.

Reading columns now use left alignment and a ragged right edge throughout web and print. Long words may break instead of overflowing, and print references use URI-aware line breaking. Web links can wrap safely, with language-aware hyphenation in prose. The two-column format, text and print specifications remain intact.
