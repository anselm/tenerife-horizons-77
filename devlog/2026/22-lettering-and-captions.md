# Lettering and captions

Recorded October 27, 2026.

Anselm still found the large Bookman cut too modern in feeling. Historical
lineage alone had not made the setting feel right. The new display cuts use
Fraunces at a large optical size with maximum softness and irregular forms,
closer to the Cooper, Windsor and Souvenir mood in the Gertrude reference.
The provenance identifies this as a contemporary interpretation; it does not
claim that Fraunces existed in 1977. Quieter Bookman headings remain.

The web audit found all nineteen main figure captions below their images.
The print audit exposed a more subtle problem: centred contained images could
have their captions aligned to the full page width, leaving the words outside
the actual image edge. Captions now use the rendered image rectangle. The
screening and working-note illustrations also gain explicit captions beneath
the images, so adjacent prose reads as a note rather than a displaced caption.

Validation: all 37 web figure captions sit below their images; desktop and
390-pixel layouts have no horizontal overflow. The 44-page PDFs retain all
159 article paragraphs and pass safe-margin checks.
