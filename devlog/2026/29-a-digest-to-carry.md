# A digest to carry

31 October 2026

Anselm selected ten Mixam PUR-bound digest copies: 5.5 × 8.5 inches, 48 colour interior pages on 50lb uncoated paper, and a separate outside-only 80lb uncoated cover. The cart specifies a 0.14-inch spine. The new digest is separately generated; the Letter edition and web folios remain intact.

The type reflows into two columns, including the editorial desks previously set in three. A taller working canvas makes useful room at the digest aspect ratio; scaling is uniform, so letters and pictures are not stretched. Mirrored margins reserve half an inch at the glued edge. Main body is approximately 8 pt. Three closing note pages keep the provenance readable at about 8 pt instead of the initial proof’s roughly 6.3 pt. A half-title occupies interior page 1, maintaining existing article and contents references.

The outside cover is a single wrap with a blank narrow spine, exactly 11.39 × 8.75 inches including eighth-inch bleed. A separate 52-page reading proof includes both blank inside covers. The shop instructions distinguish the two production files from the proof. No print purchase or upload is made.

Checks cover every article paragraph and the full provenance, page boxes, glue margins, embedded type, unique illustration placements and visual proofs.
