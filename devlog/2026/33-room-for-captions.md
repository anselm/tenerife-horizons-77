# Room for captions

31 October 2026

A wrapped caption beneath the ocean workshop diagram collided with the following paragraph. The illustration had a fixed allowance below it; the caption was measured but its height was not used to position the prose.

Print illustrations now use the actual caption bottom plus a clear gap. Supporting notes measure the complete stack before drawing, fall back to a side-by-side arrangement when needed, and reserve room for captions above the footer. The same rule applies to article plates, the horse, Alexander’s excursion and the editorial assembly pages. No prose has been cut to make the correction.
