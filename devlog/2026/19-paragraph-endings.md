# Paragraph endings

Recorded October 23, 2026.

Anselm noticed single words stranded on lines of their own. The issue needed another pass at composition, not another layer of decoration.

Paragraphs now request the browser’s more careful line breaking; display headings and decks use balanced wrapping. Short closing word pairs stay together in the longer prose paragraphs, without changing the writing or replacing inline links. This allows the same text to compose at different screen widths instead of fixing one desktop line break into the manuscript.

Very long closing pairs remain free to wrap to avoid forcing overflow on narrow pages. The aged paper and fold are unchanged.
