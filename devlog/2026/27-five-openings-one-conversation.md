# Five openings, one conversation

31 October 2026

Anselm asked for the editorial variety to run through the magazine rather than arrive as a supplement at the end. The kitchen interview now follows the Spanish land paper, the letters follow the ocean discussion, and the provisional accounts follow energy. The production desk alone remains near the closing pages. Earlier notes acquire brief questions, replies and disagreements, including the teaching table, holiday watering and a chair in a passage.

The opening translations are replaced by four original contributions: Spanish on land, water and the right to stay; French on testing several possible futures; Italian on communal life and private space; German on repair, parts and shared technical knowledge. Each has its own diagram, byline and subject. The English editorial remains the invitation. The imagined gathering and its readers move easily between languages; the text no longer explains a repeated translation scheme.

The kitchen interview alternates Spanish questions and answers with new English observations of the amendments, rather than translating each answer. Source names now say openingFeatures instead of editorialTranslations. Existing route keys are retained for old links. Contents and page references are repaginated in both editions. The lengths remain 52 web pages and 48 print pages.
