# The digest comes first

2026-10-31

The small book became the principal edition. Empty areas were treated as editorial
space: larger plates and body type first, then a few reading clippings, letters,
workshop arguments and practical notices. Machado walks beside the slope paper;
Hughes and Snyder accompany conservation; Rilke travels with the departures.
An original Arabic poem leaves a place at the common table.

The additions share the web reading flow, while the digest keeps 48 interior pages
and its existing 0.14-inch cover specification. Old marginal notes join the new
panels instead of repeating them. A new tensegrity drawing makes room for the
Snelson–Fuller working exchange, including its complicated question of credit.
Amber Case’s acknowledgment is kept direct: shared projects and calm technology.
The closing credits also recognize Fauna Foundation and Jane Goodall’s support,
and the Marine Megafauna Foundation, Andrea Marshall and Simon Pierce as diving
inspirations.

These are revisions made today, not backdated stages of production.
