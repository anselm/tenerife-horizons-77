# A copy to carry

Recorded October 27, 2026.

The magazine needed to leave the screen. Anselm wanted a dozen copies for an
event that evening, with a local print shop doing the rush job and the option
to handle the staples himself.

The web sheets were not print pages. A separate Letter-size edition now fixes
the columns, page breaks, contents and folios. White paper replaces simulated
aging and the screen fold. A frontispiece, two contents pages, the complete
issue, final provenance notes and a back cover make 52 pages.

Two PDFs support the same content: normal reader order for a print shop, and
26 imposed 11 × 17 sides for duplex output, folding and saddle stitching.
Each copy takes 13 tabloid sheets. The accompanying brief specifies twelve
collated copies, a folded proof first, and a side-bound Letter fallback.

Checks: every page rendered and inspected in contact sheets, selected pages
at larger size, no text outside the safe margins, all 159 article paragraphs
found in extracted text, embedded document fonts and verified 52/26 page counts.
A physical proof remains necessary to confirm printer orientation and paper.
