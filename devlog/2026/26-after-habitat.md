# After Habitat

31 October 2026

The post-conference issue of *Jericho*, the Habitat ’76 newspaper, provided a concrete reference for editorial density: narrow columns, large headlines, boxed notices, interviews and mundane unfinished business beside immense ambitions. The Tenerife edition now includes four new pages: a Spanish/English kitchen interview, provisional accounts in pesetas, letters and objections, and the closing desk. Local voices use Spanish; the conference’s five languages do not imply universal fluency among workers. Carmen Pérez and the correspondents are fictional. No real testimony is claimed.

The old fish diagram had been placed four times. It is no longer used. Three marine notes now have distinct, purposeful plates: habitat services, four intervals at one window, and blackwater forms without species identification. A receiving-court study belongs to the glider note. Other leaves become notices, corrections or cuttings instead of mandatory illustration slots. The remaining duplicate filler drawings and the repeated terrace back-cover image are removed. Each placed image is used once.

Ten original SVG plates were drawn for these specific contexts, including a small editorial cartoon, kitchen circulation, accounts, a paste-up board, and two distinct Alexander notebook studies. No Habitat image is reproduced. The web edition has 52 pages; print has 48, with a 12-sheet tabloid imposition and a separately prepared reader-spreads file. The contents, folios, cross-references and shop brief are revised together.
