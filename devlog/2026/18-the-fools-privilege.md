# The fool’s privilege

Recorded October 23, 2026.

Gilliam needed more room to tease the gathering, without making his feature longer or importing the reputation of films he had not yet made. The revised report gives him a few invented remarks: an embarrassing cupboard, an administrative tower beside the aircraft lift, and somewhere to sulk in the communal settlement.

The deeper thread is the fool’s permission to examine authority, carnival’s borrowed crown, and the unruly bodies of Bosch and Bruegel. Dürer, Doré, Carroll and Fellini enter as editorial artistic affinities rather than a fabricated account of Gilliam listing his influences. His own pretensions are available for inspection too. He remains a fellow dreamer, laughing from inside the drawing.

The final edition notes explicitly identify the dialogue as invented. No new authentic quotation or claim of historical attendance is implied.
