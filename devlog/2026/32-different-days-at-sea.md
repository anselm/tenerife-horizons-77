# Different days at sea

31 October 2026

The coastal notebook had mixed one long afternoon with several other experiences, then repeated its heading across an automatic halfway break. Anselm clarified the distinction: Montaña Amarilla, barracuda and an afternoon without returning to shore; other days in a Zodiac, making a dolphin friend, turtles and rays, swimming with pilot whales, and blackwater dives.

The first page now completes the afternoon. “Other days, other waters” begins the wider notebook, including calima, cliffside houses, winter expatriates and the borrowed words of friends. An explicit editorial break keeps that distinction in both web and print without adding pages. The island atlas brings in practising Silbo Gomero, the shepherd’s pole and tallow, La Geria’s vine hollows, and Pliny’s dog-name account. Supporting references and the modern pilot-whale estimate stay in the closing notes.
