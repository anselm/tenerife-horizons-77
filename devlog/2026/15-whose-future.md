# Whose future?

A search for a voice whose 1977 was truly pivotal led to Wangari Maathai and the founding of the Green Belt Movement. Her feature asks about water, work, tending and who returns after the visitors leave. Habib Rahman’s dismissal from Delhi’s Urban Art Commission gives a shorter contribution its difficult question: who can refuse a bad proposal? The two pieces alter the conference’s conversation rather than simply adding names to a roster.
