# A magazine in several hands

20 November 2026, later revision. This follows the [three-feature restoration](../20261120-restored-spreads/README.md); it does not pretend that work happened on a different date.

The criticism was structural: a capital headline, summary and generic drawing kept reappearing. Changing three features did not change the book's governing habit. The new working conceit is several groups bringing distinct contributions. The [current compass](../20261120-editorial-and-design-compass.md) supersedes the single-editor premise. [Anselm's dreamers note](../20261120-the-dreamers.md) remains the emotional anchor.

## The concrete revision

The digest has 64 interior pages, including one blank endpaper, and a 68-page whole-book proof. The web has 63 independently numbered folios. French collage crosses digest 6–7 / web 8–9; the film-desk castle crosses digest 48–49 / web 52–53. A photographic Drago leaf faces the German essay in print. English, Spanish, Italian, ecology, finance, energy and office contributions use distinct typographic arrangements. Most redundant top-of-article and supporting wireframes are retired; the service section, glider sequence and landing study keep explanatory jobs. Existing art remains in source history and assets.

Three new bitmap artworks were made with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. [Exact prompts](art-prompts.json) record the commissions. Saved plates: [coastal collage](../../assets/dreaming-together-collage.png), [film-desk castle](../../assets/film-desk-castle.png), [Drago photograph](../../assets/drago-excursion-halftone.png). Body text and titles remain editable type. The new plates are not authentic archival evidence; the endnotes say so. Reference images supplied in the conversation informed composition but were not republished as our artwork.

## Preservation and inspection

All 198 source essay/report paragraphs were checked against extracted PDF text. The carried reading panels, poem lines and their commentary are also checked; Arabic is visually reviewed in its embedded font. New composed text/image rectangles do not collide. Trim and media boxes, correct left/right spread parity, embedded fonts, blank inside covers and the final blank endpaper were checked. Every page was reviewed in contact sheets; the panoramas and their typography were inspected separately. Desktop and 390-pixel mobile previews were checked; mobile paragraphs now sit below the artwork rather than over it.

The two panoramic images provide about 127 pixels/inch at full spread width. Their grain and cutout/ink treatment are deliberate, but inspect the small ink detail in a physical proof before ordering. This is not a claim of 300 dpi photographic source art. Cover design remains from the preceding edition; its old 0.14-inch production wrap is **not valid** for 64 pages. A new Mixam template is required.

## Scope left open

The surrounding Alexander, atlas, programme and document leaves keep their earlier body compositions with serif headings. The cover has not been redesigned in this pass. The print builder still adapts the older renderer, with explicit contributor compositions layered in; the revised page map is generated, not hand-maintained. Future iterations should judge adjacent spreads and the physical object, not protect existing assets because they took time to make.
