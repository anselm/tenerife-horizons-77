FINISHING
Fold the stack at the centre, nest all 13 sheets, and saddle stitch twice.
Self-finishing: a long-arm or saddle stapler is needed to reach the fold.
The outside sheet is the first pair of PDF sides. Nest subsequent sheets
inside it in their printed order. Trimming is optional; margins are generous.
